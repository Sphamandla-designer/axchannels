Capabilities

From digital experiences to intelligent systems.

Four connected capabilities, one way of thinking: understand the system, then design the experience it deserves.

Digital Experiences

01

The journeys customers and users move through. We design experiences around how people actually think and decide — so the path through your business becomes its clearest part.

  • Experience & journey designMapping and redesigning the paths people take through your business.
  • UX/UI designInterfaces with hierarchy, rhythm and one clear next step.
  • Service & experience architectureThe structure beneath the journey — channels, touchpoints, handoffs.
Both. Many engagements start with an existing journey that has grown tangled. We map what exists, find where people get lost, and redesign from the evidence.
We validate before we build: prototypes are tested with the people who will use them, and design decisions trace back to what we observed — not to taste.

Digital Products

02

Products designed as coherent ecosystems — from first concept to interface systems that scale. Product thinking first; screens follow.

  • Product strategy & definitionFraming the problem, the users and what the product must do first.
  • Product interface & design systemsConsistent, scalable component systems — not one-off screens.
  • Web & mobile product designFull product design across devices, built for real use.

That’s exactly when to start. Definition is the first stage of our approach — understanding and framing the problem before any interface is drawn.
Yes. Enablement is part of the process: build-ready design systems, specifications and support through implementation, so the design survives contact with delivery.

Business Systems

03

The operational control centre. We design internal systems — dashboards, workflows, data structures — that make operations legible, trackable and controllable.

  • Operational dashboards & toolingThe state of the operation, visible at a glance.
  • Workflow & process system designSystems shaped around how work actually happens.
  • Information architecture for dataStructuring the data a business runs on so it can be used.

With the spreadsheets — they encode how your business really works. We map the operation as it is, then design the system that deserves to replace them.
No — it’s design. We define the system your operation actually needs. Sometimes that shapes a custom build; sometimes it makes an existing platform finally fit.

AI & Intelligent Experiences

04

Where human judgement meets machine intelligence. We design AI into products and systems deliberately — assisting decisions, surfacing insight, keeping people in control.

  • AI experience & interaction designIntelligence people can understand, question and trust.
  • Intelligent workflows & assistanceAutomating the repetitive; sharpening the judgement calls.
  • Human-in-the-loop decision designAI recommends. People decide. The interface makes that explicit.

See it applied — FINOS intelligence layer →

Yes — and sometimes the honest answer is “not yet, and not there.” An AI opportunity assessment looks at your decisions and data first, and recommends intelligence only where it genuinely earns its place.
By design: intelligence is always visible as a suggestion with its reasoning, reversible, and subordinate to a human decision point. Trust is an interface property as much as a model property.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information AX-Channels collects through this website, why we process it, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is written to meet the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

AX-Channels is a digital design studio based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Under POPIA every responsible party must register an Information Officer with the Information Regulator. If that registration has not been completed, it should be done before this policy is published.

2. What we collect

Only two things, and the first one is entirely up to you.

What you send us

If you complete an enquiry form on this site, email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive whatever you put in it. The forms ask for:

  • your first and last name;
  • your email address;
  • the service you are interested in, and your budget range, where you choose to select them;
  • a description of your project, in your own words.

Nothing on this site asks for an identity number, a date of birth, banking details, health information or any other special personal information as POPIA defines it. Please do not send those through the enquiry forms.

What our host records

This site is served by a web host that keeps standard server logs, which typically include your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested and your browser type. We do not have a dashboard onto those logs and we do not use them to build a profile of you.

3. Why we process it, and on what basis

We use what you send us to reply to you, to quote on work, and to run a project if you go ahead. Under section 11 of POPIA our grounds are:

  • Your consent. You choose to fill in a form and send it.
  • Performance of a contract. Where we are quoting on or delivering work for you.
  • Our legitimate interests. Keeping a record of enquiries and correspondence so we can answer you properly.

We do not sell your information, we do not rent it, and we do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising pixels, no session recording and no cross-site tracking, and it stores nothing in your browser. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Two qualifications, so this is complete:

  • The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes a request to Google's servers, and that request carries your IP address. Google states that it does not use Google Fonts requests to build advertising profiles, but the request happens and you should know about it.
  • If you click through to WhatsApp or LinkedIn, you leave this site and that company's own privacy policy applies from that point.

Some of the services above are operated by companies outside South Africa, so your information may be processed abroad. Section 72 of POPIA permits this where the receiving country, or a binding agreement with the operator, offers protection substantially similar to POPIA. Before launch, each provider's data-processing terms should be checked against that test. [confirm this check has been done for each provider named above]

8. How we protect it

Section 19 of POPIA requires reasonable technical and organisational measures. In practice, for this website: it is served over HTTPS, it stores no personal information itself, and enquiries go to a mailbox rather than to a database on this site. Access to that mailbox is limited to the people who need it.

We do not claim any security certification, and we do not claim that any system is immune to compromise. If a breach affects your personal information, section 22 of POPIA requires us to notify both you and the Information Regulator, and we will.

9. Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and be given a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, misleading or obtained unlawfully;
  • object to our processing on reasonable grounds;
  • withdraw a consent you have given, without affecting anything done before you withdrew it;
  • complain to the Information Regulator; and
  • go to court over an alleged breach.

To exercise any of these, email info@axchannels.co.za. A formal access request under the Promotion of Access to Information Act is made on the Regulator's prescribed form, and a fee may apply. We will respond within the period the legislation allows.

10. Complaints

Please raise a complaint with us first, so we can try to put it right. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. Its current contact details and complaint forms are published on inforegulator.org.za (opens in a new tab).

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. If a change materially affects how we handle information you have already given us, we will tell you directly.

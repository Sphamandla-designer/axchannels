About AX-Channels

We Design Digital Experiences Around People And Business, Combining UX Research, Interface Design And Business Thinking.

We bring UX research, structure and interface design together, so what gets built is decided before it is drawn. Every decision balances what a user needs to accomplish with what the business needs to achieve.

From websites and mobile interfaces to dashboards and brand systems, the pieces connect into one experience — easier to understand, easier to use, and built to keep performing after launch.

Why Businesses Choose AX-Channels

The Commitment

A reply in two working days, and a real date in writing.

Every enquiry gets an answer within two working days. Every proposal carries a timeline, a scope and a price, worked out after discovery rather than guessed before it. Website packages start at R2,999, and anything quoted against scope is quoted in writing.

UX decides the layout

Research and structure come first, interface second. A layout drawn before the architecture is a guess with a colour scheme, and it is the visitor who pays for it.

02 02

You see it at every stage

Five stages, and work is shown at the end of each one rather than saved for a reveal. Direction gets agreed while it is still cheap to change.

03 03

You own what we build

Sites are handed over on a CMS with admin access and a walk-through. Maintenance is free after launch and optional after that, never a condition of keeping your own site running.

Built to be found

The SEO structure is mapped alongside the UX architecture, before anything is drawn, then the build is tuned for speed, devices and search.

Five Stages, With Work Shown At The End Of Each

You see progress at every stage rather than only at the finish, so direction is agreed as we go.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Research

    The business, the people it serves and what success looks like, before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Structure & UX Architecture

    Journeys, information architecture and, for websites, the SEO structure the pages are mapped to.

  3. 03

    Design & Brand Integration

    The interface, designed onto that structure, with your brand carried through rather than applied on top.

  4. 04

    Build & Optimise

    Built for speed and tested across devices where the engagement includes development, or handed over ready to build.

  5. 05

    Launch & Monitor

    Launch, then a watch on how it performs — and what to improve next.

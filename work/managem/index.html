ManaGem

A management workspace: work, people and progress in one legible place.

The problem

Where the complexity lived

Management information lived across tools that didn’t speak to each other. The real status of work was assembled by hand — week after week — and was out of date the moment it was finished.

Managing the work took more work than the work.

The response

What we designed

We started from the team’s actual workflow — how work arrives, moves and completes — and designed one workspace around it instead of asking the team to adapt to another tool.

Work, people and progress share one structure, so status is a view, not a weekly project.

  • One workspace shaped around the team’s real workflow
  • Work, people and progress in a single structure
  • Status as a live view instead of a manual report
  • An interface calm enough to live in all day
Before — the weekly assembly
After — one workspace

The outcome

Complexity, made usable

ManaGem was designed to make the state of the work visible without ceremony — the picture assembles itself, and the team spends its attention on the work instead of on reporting it.

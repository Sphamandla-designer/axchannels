WasteMart

An operational system for recycling: collection, tracking and processing designed as one legible flow.

LogisticsBusiness SystemOperationsMobile
SectorRecycling / Logistics
EngagementBusiness system & experience design
CapabilitiesSystems · Experience · Mobile

The problem

Where the complexity lived

Recycling operations moved faster than the information about them. Coordination happened through calls, paper and memory; records were disconnected from the ground truth. Nobody could see the whole flow — only their fragment of it.

The operation worked. Seeing it didn’t.

The response

What we designed

We mapped the real operational flow first — depot, routes, pickups, processing — and designed the system around those workflows rather than around forms.

The result is a shared operational picture: a control view for the depot, a focused mobile view for drivers, and data captured once at the point of work instead of re-typed afterwards.

  • An operations dashboard that shows the whole flow at a glance
  • Workflow design matched to how collection actually happens
  • A driver-first mobile view: the next pickup, not a form
  • Tracking from request to processing in one record
Workflow — collection to processing

The outcome

Complexity, made usable

WasteMart was designed so the operation and the information about it finally move together — one flow that everyone can see, from the depot dashboard to the driver’s hand.
